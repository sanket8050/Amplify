03 — Contact Intelligence
Who to Contact First
Founder / CEO
  • Best for companies with <20 engineers where CEO still codes or reads technical context
  • Best platforms: LinkedIn, HN replies, Twitter/X for technical founders
  • When: company is pre-Series A, or you have a specific insight about their product
  • Risk: CEO is busy — your message must be sharp, short, and show you've used their product
  • Companies to use this on: Weflow (Philipp), Gridmatic (Kevin), Topliner, BoWatt, SuppMatch
CTO / Engineering Head
  • Best for companies with 20–80 engineers where CTO still does code review
  • CTOs respond to technical specificity — mention their stack, their architecture challenges
  • Often more reachable than CEO because they're less bombarded
  • Reference GitHub repos, their blog posts, or HN comments they've made
  • Companies to use this on: Goody (Mark), Karat Financial (Will Kim), GovSignals, Kanmon
Engineering Manager
  • Best when company has 50–150 engineers and there's a clear eng manager layer
  • They care most about execution speed, communication, and team fit
  • Find them via LinkedIn — search "[company] engineering manager"
  • Message should reference specific projects or pull requests, and ask a thoughtful question
  • Companies to use this on: Ashby, Cosuno, Estuary, Upway, Cyble
Recruiter / Talent
  • Only after applying via the portal — recruiter follow-up increases response rate by ~40%
  • Never reach out to a recruiter as your first touch — go to engineers first
  • Recruiters respond to: clear role fit, availability, and no ambiguity about experience
  • Find them via LinkedIn: "[company] recruiter" or "[company] talent acquisition"
  • Good for: Ashby, Ruby Labs, Cyble, Enrollio
04 — Playbook
Outreach Strategy by Company Type
Tiny Startup (<15 people)
APPROACH: FOUNDER OUTREACH
01
Find the founder on LinkedIn. Check if they've posted in the last 30 days — if yes, comment genuinely on their post first.
02
Message format: "Hey [Name] — I've been following [Company] and actually built something similar ([brief PRISM mention]). I'm a full-stack engineer with [tech] who ships production systems. Would love to chat if you're open to it."
03
Keep it under 4 sentences. No resume link in first message. Link to GitHub or live project instead.
04
If no reply in 5 days, send one follow-up: "Totally fine if timing's off — wanted to leave you a [GitHub link] to my recent work in case it's useful."
05
Best companies: Kanmon, BoWatt, Topliner, Matcha.fm, Pinetree, SuppMatch, VidRush
Growth Startup (15–80 people)
APPROACH: CTO/ENG LEAD OUTREACH
01
Apply via careers page FIRST. Then immediately connect with the CTO/eng head on LinkedIn.
02
Message format: "Just applied for the [Role] at [Company]. I've shipped full-stack production systems with [specific tech from their JD] — here's [one specific thing] that matches what you're building. Happy to share more."
03
Reference something specific from their tech blog, GitHub, or HN thread. Shows genuine research.
04
Best companies: Weflow, Cosuno, Hotglue, GovSignals, Karat Financial, Goody, Estuary
YC / VC-Backed (open roles)
